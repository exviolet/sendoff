Pattern lab — one cell, many states

Every background below is derived from a single unit: the monospace character cell the editor itself is built on. Variety comes from the state of that lattice, not from unrelated decoration.

Progression down the page

Instead of repeating one pattern, the lattice thins as you scroll: dense at the top, dots in the middle, bare baselines at the end. Scrolling then reads as moving from a blank document toward a sent one.
